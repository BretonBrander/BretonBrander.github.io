Support

How can we help?

For support, use the Brander Design contact form.

For bugs, questions, or feature ideas, include the device you are using, its software version, the experience involved, and what you expected to feel.

Why can’t I feel haptics?

Haptics require a physical iPhone or Apple Watch. Simulators can display the interface but cannot reproduce the device’s tactile feedback. Also check the Haptics setting inside the experience.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Spring Windup, Momentum Wheel, Infinite Scroll, and Stretch all include Apple Watch versions with touch, Digital Crown interactions where appropriate, and on-wrist feedback.

Does Tactile Fidget collect data?

No. The app does not collect or transmit personal data. Read the privacy policy for details.
