Privacy Policy

Your interactions stay yours.

Effective date: July 11, 2026

Data collection

Tactile Fidget does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, developer-operated server, or third-party data-collection SDK.

On-device activity

Fidget interactions, motion settings, and usage totals remain on your iPhone or Apple Watch. They are used only to operate the app and are not sent to the developer or third parties.

Device services

The app uses Apple-provided system features for haptics, sound, touch input, and Digital Crown input. These features operate on the device.

Support contact

If you choose to contact support through the external Brander Design contact form, the information you voluntarily provide is handled by that website and its service providers. Tactile Fidget itself does not transmit support information.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the Tactile Fidget support page.
